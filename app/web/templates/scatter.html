How Scatter pays

Land 8 or more of the same symbol anywhere on the 6×5 board. Winners burst, the rest tumble down, new symbols fall in, and it goes again until nothing pays.

Scatter pays anywhere and is never removed by a tumble. 4+ at the end of a spin pay {% for n, v in scatter_pay.items() %}{{ n }}{{ '+' if loop.last else '' }} = {{ '%g' % (v / 100) }}x{{ ', ' if not loop.last else '' }}{% endfor %} and start 10 free spins. During free spins, 3+ add 5 more.

Multiplier orbs (x2 up to x100) fall like any symbol. When a spin ends with a win, the orbs on the board are added up and multiply that spin's win. In free spins they are kept: the multiplier grows for the rest of the bonus and multiplies every win.

One round, including any free spins, pays at most {{ cfg.max_win_x }}x your bet. Return to player: about {{ cfg.rtp }}%, measured by simulation. Rounds are final once dealt. Wins smaller than your bet are shown quietly on purpose: they are not a profit.
