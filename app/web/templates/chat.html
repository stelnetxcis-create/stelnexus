What “experimental” means

  • Short-poll delivery. New messages arrive within about four seconds; this is not a websocket feed yet.
  • Best-effort presence. The room list and the typing dots come from live sessions and in-process memory, so they can lag or reset.
  • Everything else is production. Messages are stored, permission-checked (chat.use), CSRF-protected and rate-limited like the rest of the console.
